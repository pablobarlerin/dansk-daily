# Installing Dansk Daily on your iPhone

The app lives at: https://pablobarlerin.github.io/dansk-daily/

Your iPhone may be set to Spanish. Where that matters, the Spanish name is in brackets.

## 1. Put it on your Home Screen

1. Open **Safari** on your iPhone. It must be Safari, not another browser.
2. Go to https://pablobarlerin.github.io/dansk-daily/
3. Tap the **Share** button (Compartir), the square with an arrow pointing up.
4. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen** (Añadir a pantalla de inicio).
5. Tap **Add** (Añadir).

## 2. Open it from the icon, and save the audio

1. Close Safari. Open Dansk Daily **from its new icon** on the Home Screen. Do not use Safari from now on. The icon version keeps its own separate copy of your progress.
2. Connect to **Wi-Fi**.
3. Tap **Settings** in the app. Find the line **Audio saved for offline**.
4. Keep the app open until it says all the audio is saved, when the two numbers match.
5. After that, the app works without internet.

## 3. A Danish voice (optional)

You only need this for the big numbers. If you want it:

1. Open **Settings** (Ajustes) on the iPhone.
2. Go to **Accessibility** (Accesibilidad), then **Spoken Content** (Contenido leído), then **Voices** (Voces).
3. Choose **Danish** (Danés) and download a voice.

(The names of these menus may differ a little between iOS versions.)

## 4. A daily reminder

Practising for 30 seconds a day works better than a long session once in a while. Set a reminder:

- **Reminders** (Recordatorios): make a new reminder called "Dansk Daily", set it to repeat every day, or
- **Clock** (Reloj): open the **Alarm** (Alarma) tab, tap +, pick a time and a label such as "Dansk Daily".

## 5. The weekly update

Once a week, open your Claude Code assistant on your computer and give it these three things:

1. A message saying **add this week**.
2. A screenshot of your schedule for the week.
3. Your reports. In the app, open **Settings** and tap **Copy reports** (under "Content"). Then paste the text into your message.

The assistant adds the new week and puts the updated app online. Before you start, it is a good idea to **Export backup** (see step 6).

When the assistant says the app is updated, open Dansk Daily from its Home Screen icon while on **Wi-Fi**. It picks up the new version and saves the new audio. Check **Audio saved for offline** in Settings until the two numbers match.

## 6. Back up your progress

In **Settings**, under "Your progress":

- **Export backup**: makes a file with all your progress. A **Save backup file** link appears. Tap it and save it to **Files** (Archivos), or send it to yourself.
- **Import backup**: loads a backup file you saved earlier. Use it if you get a new iPhone or have to install the app again.

Make a backup about once a month. The app can remind you ("Monthly backup reminder").

## Warning

**If you delete the icon from your Home Screen, or clear Safari website data, all your progress is erased.** Always tap **Export backup** and save the file first.
